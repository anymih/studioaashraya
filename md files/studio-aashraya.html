What the training changes

An IIT architect on your site means numbers get checked.

The degree is where the habit starts. The habit is to check a number before trusting it. Studio Aashraya applies it to the three things clients usually have to take on faith.

Structure

The architect reads the structural drawings and does not leave them to a bystander. Column sizes, bar spacing and concrete grade are checked against the drawing before every pour.

Quantities

Every design becomes a bill of quantities before construction begins. You see where the money goes, item by item and rate by rate, before the first brick.

Records

Each stage closes with a written checklist and dated photographs. You can read the history of your house at any point in the build.

Method

Nothing is poured, closed or covered until the last stage is checked.

Eight stages, from the first plot visit to handover. Four of them end in a gate, which is a written check by the architect.

  1. Plot review

    Setbacks, ground coverage, FAR, plot shape and approvals for your plot, written up before you commit to anything.

  2. Design and quantities

    Architectural drawings, structural coordination and a bill of quantities that prices the design item by item.

  3. Approvals

    Sanction drawings prepared and followed through with the local authority.

  4. Foundation

    Excavation, soil at founding level, reinforcement and cover are checked before concrete goes in.

    Gate

  5. Plinth and structure

    Column and slab reinforcement, shuttering levels and concrete mix are checked before each pour.

    Gate

  6. Services

    Electrical conduits, plumbing lines and drainage are tested before walls are closed.

    Gate

  7. Finishes

    Waterproofing is tested before tiling. Joinery, paint and fittings are checked room by room against the drawing.

    Gate

  8. Handover

    A snag list closed room by room, as-built drawings and the warranty terms in writing.

Questions

Before you call.

Who is responsible if something goes wrong on site?

The studio is. The architect who drew the house also builds it, so the drawing, the quantities and the work on site sit under one agreement.

Which areas do you build in?

New plots in Gurugram and the rest of Delhi NCR. Completed homes so far are in Patna, Delhi and Almora.

What will it cost?

We quote after a plot review, because cost depends on the plot, the number of floors, the structure and the level of finish. The quote comes with a bill of quantities so you can see how it is built up.

Can I manage the build from abroad?

Yes. Stage reports, dated photographs and a video walkthrough at each gate are part of the work for every client.
